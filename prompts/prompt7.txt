## Features

- Stock balance.
- Stock in.
- Stock out.
- Opening stock.
- Stock adjustment.
- Inventory movement history.
- Low-stock alerts.
- Stock valuation architecture.
- Search.
- Filtering.
- Inventory reports.

## Important Architecture

Inventory changes must be traceable.

Do not allow arbitrary stock balance editing.

Implement inventory movements with:
- Product.
- Movement type.
- Quantity.
- Reference.
- Date/time.
- User.
- Notes.

Stock balance must be calculated or maintained reliably from validated stock movements.

Use database transactions where appropriate.

Prevent negative stock according to the configured business rules.

Protect stock adjustment operations with permissions.

Create backend services for inventory operations.

Do not allow the frontend to directly change inventory balance.
