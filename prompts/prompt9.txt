## Transaction Integrity

Checkout must be atomic.

The system must:
1. Validate products.
2. Validate stock.
3. Validate prices.
4. Validate discounts.
5. Calculate totals on the backend.
6. Validate payment.
7. Create sale records.
8. Create sale item records.
9. Record payment.
10. Deduct stock using the inventory service.
11. Commit the transaction.

If an operation fails, do not leave partial sales records or incorrect inventory.

Do not trust totals calculated only in React.
